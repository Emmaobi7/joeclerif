# Antigravity Build Brief — Joclarif Digital Hospitality Ecosystem

## PROMPT 1 — PROJECT FOUNDATION AND CREATIVE DIRECTION

You are working on a premium digital product proposal for Joclarif Hotel, Zaria, Nigeria.

The objective is to build a visually impressive, interactive, single-page proposal microsite that presents a vision for transforming Joclarif's digital hospitality experience.

This is NOT the actual hotel website or production booking platform. It is a mock proposal intended to be demonstrated to hotel ownership and management to win their interest in a larger custom software development project.

### Product vision

**Joclarif Digital Hospitality Ecosystem**

A connected hospitality platform spanning:

- Guest-facing website.
- Guest mobile application.
- Hotel management environment.
- Central reservation and availability engine.
- AI-powered guest experience.
- Integrated operational services.

### Technology direction

Use a modern, maintainable, production-quality frontend architecture.

Preferred direction:
- Next.js.
- TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS or an appropriately structured styling system.
- Lucide icons.
- Reusable components.
- Responsive design.

Inspect the existing repository before making architectural decisions. Preserve useful existing configuration and avoid unnecessary dependencies.

Do not introduce WordPress, generic hotel templates, or purchased themes.

### Creative direction

The design must communicate luxury, confidence, sophistication and technological maturity.

Think premium hospitality brand combined with a modern enterprise technology presentation.

Visual characteristics:
- Elegant editorial typography.
- Beautiful hotel and hospitality photography.
- Deep forest green, warm ivory, champagne and restrained bronze accents.
- Spacious layouts.
- Strong typography hierarchy.
- Refined motion and transitions.
- Beautiful cards and subtle borders.
- Excellent desktop and mobile responsiveness.

Avoid:
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics throughout the entire page.
- Excessive gradients.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Cartoon illustrations.
- Generic AI-generated robot imagery.
- Overloaded layouts.
- Cheap-looking animations.
- Excessive rounded cards.
- Template-like sections.

The result should look deliberately art-directed.

### Important factual restrictions

Joclarif is a real hotel in Zaria, Nigeria.

We have identified an expired historical website and its former Romancy WordPress theme. We are NOT rebuilding that website.

Do not invent:
- Official Joclarif branding guidelines.
- Verified room categories.
- Actual room prices.
- Actual hall capacities.
- Actual room availability.
- Existing hotel software.
- Existing payment integrations.
- Confirmed operational capabilities.
- Hotel ownership endorsements.

Where information is not verified, use clearly labelled illustrative examples or proposed capabilities.

Do not present this proposal as an officially approved Joclarif product.

### First task

Inspect the repository and establish the project structure.

Then create a concise implementation plan covering:
1. Page architecture.
2. Component structure.
3. Design system.
4. Interactive demonstrations.
5. Responsive strategy.

Do not begin with a generic landing page. Build the experience around the proposal's actual business and technical story.
