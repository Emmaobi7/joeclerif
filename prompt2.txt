## PROMPT 2 — BUILD THE ONE-PAGE PROPOSAL

Now implement the Joclarif Digital Hospitality Ecosystem proposal microsite.

This must be ONE cohesive, scrollable page, not a collection of unrelated pages.

The page should feel like a premium product presentation that a hotel owner can explore independently.

### 1. Hero — Introduce the vision

Brand: JOCLARIF

Main headline:

"One destination. Every stay."

Supporting statement:

"A connected hospitality experience bringing reservations, guest services and hotel operations into one intelligent digital ecosystem."

Include:
- Strong luxury hospitality photography.
- A premium visual composition.
- A clear explanation of the proposed transformation.
- Navigation that scrolls to the relevant sections.
- A subtle scroll indicator.

Avoid claiming this is Joclarif's existing digital platform.

A wedding organiser requests 15 rooms for family members and a hall for the same event.

Use a clean visual diagram to communicate this.

### 5. Conversational Booking Co-Pilot

This is a major differentiator.

Demonstrate how a guest can type:

"I need 15 rooms for my family coming from Lagos for a wedding, plus a hall for November 25th."

The assistant should:
- Interpret the request.
- Extract dates, quantities and booking types.
- Identify missing information.
- Ask follow-up questions.
- Check room inventory through the booking engine.
- Check hall availability.
- Present suitable options.
- Assemble a combined quotation.

Important: AI interprets requests and coordinates deterministic booking services. It must never fabricate prices, availability or booking confirmations.

Create an interactive prototype rather than merely describing the feature.

### 6. AI Concierge and live human support

Demonstrate:

- 24/7 routine guest assistance.
- Hotel information enquiries.
- Facility information.
- Room service enquiries.
- Check-in/check-out questions.
- Multilingual communication.
- Human staff escalation.
- Conversation assignment and tracking.

Show a sample conversation where a French-speaking guest asks a question and the hotel employee sees its English translation.

Show a second example where the AI transfers a complex airport transportation enquiry to a human employee.

Do not claim a fixed percentage of enquiries will be automated.

### 7. Digital check-in and guest identity

Present a proposed digital check-in journey:

1. Open an existing reservation.
2. Give consent to identity processing.
3. Capture a passport or supported identity document.
4. Extract information using OCR.
5. Allow the guest to review the extracted information.
6. Submit for hotel verification.
7. Update the check-in workflow.

Explain that OCR extracts information but does not authenticate document genuineness.

Mention privacy, consent, encryption, access controls and retention policies.
