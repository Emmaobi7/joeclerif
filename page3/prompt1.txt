# J O C L A R I F — COMPANION
## Implementation Brief: Private Product Showcase

Build a new standalone page for **Joclarif Companion** inside the existing Joclarif project.

### 1. First: Understand the Existing Project

Before coding:
- Inspect the existing framework, routing, components, styling system, and animation/3D libraries.
- Reuse the established STILL WATERS design language.
- Preserve existing pages and functionality.
- Do not redesign the main Joclarif website.
- Create a standalone route, such as `/companion`.
- Do not add this page to the public homepage navigation or publicly advertise it. It is a private product presentation shared directly with the hotel stakeholders.

### 2. Product Positioning

**Product:** Joclarif Companion

**Headline:** A More Personal Way to Experience Hospitality.

**Supporting copy:** A thoughtfully managed companion discovery and coordination experience, designed around verified identities, personal choice, and greater confidence for everyone involved.

This is a concept for lawful adult social companionship and introductions. Do not present it as a sexual-services marketplace or build functionality intended to facilitate prostitution, exploitation, trafficking, coercion, or other unlawful activity.

The product uses a hybrid model:
- Individuals can create and manage their own profiles.
- Joclarif independently verifies and approves profiles.
- Only approved, active profiles can appear in discovery or receive requests.
- Users retain control over their availability, profile visibility, boundaries, and requests.

### 3. Visual Direction

Maintain the established STILL WATERS identity:
- Deep umber and rich dark backgrounds.
- Pale champagne typography and restrained amber-gold highlights.
- Cinematic lighting, soft reflections, subtle atmospheric gradients.
- Elegant editorial typography.
- Premium hospitality aesthetic.
- Smooth, restrained transitions and micro-interactions.

This should feel like a sophisticated private product reveal, not a generic dating website, social network, or admin dashboard.

### 4. Hero Experience

Create a cinematic hero section with:

**Left side**

- Short product description.

  - Verified identities

**Right side**

Include a discreet label: PRIVATE PRODUCT CONCEPT.

### 5. Interactive Smartphone Prototype

The phone must demonstrate two distinct experiences through a toggle:

**GUEST EXPERIENCE**

Build a realistic simulated guest journey:

1. Welcome screen.
2. Discover approved companion profiles.
3. Browse profile cards with photographs, biographies, interests, availability, and stated boundaries.
4. Open a profile to inspect its details.
5. Send a request for a selected time.
6. View request status.
7. See a simulated booking confirmation.
8. View the protected-payment status.
9. Access meeting details and safety options.
10. View the completion-confirmation screen.

Use tasteful fictional adult profile data and appropriate imagery. Do not use explicit or sexualised imagery.

**COMPANION EXPERIENCE**

Build a realistic simulated companion journey:

1. Welcome / account screen.
2. Create and edit a personal profile.
3. Set interests, introduction preferences, availability, and boundaries.
4. Submit identity verification.
5. View verification status.
6. See a clear distinction between:
   - Draft
   - Verification pending
   - Approved and active
   - Suspended / verification expired
7. View incoming requests.
8. Accept or decline a request.
9. View confirmed bookings.
10. View the protected-funds and earnings interface.
11. View completion confirmation and withdrawal eligibility.

Important: Creating an account or profile does not automatically make someone eligible to offer services. Only Joclarif-approved accounts can become discoverable or accept requests.

### 6. Trust, Verification and Safety

The product's trust model must be visible in the demonstration.

**Identity verification**
- Verify legal identity and age.
- Minimum age: 18.
- Use a reputable identity-verification provider in a real production implementation.
- Joclarif approval is required before activation.
- The individual cannot mark their own profile as verified.
- Reverification may be required when verification expires or suspicious activity is detected.

For this proposal prototype, simulate the verification journey. Do not collect real identity documents or claim a live verification integration exists.

**Companion protection**
- Control over profile visibility.
- Ability to accept or decline requests.
- Ability to block and report users.
- Clear personal boundaries.
- Access to safety support.
- Ability to cancel or report unsafe situations.

**Guest protection**
- Only approved profiles appear in discovery.
- Clear profile information and booking terms.
- Secure communication concept.
- Reporting and support.
- Transparent booking and payment status.

**Hotel protection**
- Visitor authorisation and access procedures.
- Appropriate guest and visitor records.
- Restricted access to relevant hotel areas.
- Incident reporting and escalation.
- Operational audit history.
- Only the minimum necessary information is shared with hotel staff.

Do not expose private conversations, sensitive companion profile information, or unnecessary personal data to hotel staff.

### 7. Upfront Payment and Completion Confirmation

Demonstrate the intended payment lifecycle using simulated data only.

**Booking sequence:**
1. Guest sends a request.
2. Companion accepts.
3. Guest pays upfront through the proposed payment provider.
4. The interface shows payment as secured.
5. The booking becomes confirmed.
6. The meeting takes place.
7. Both parties independently confirm completion.
8. The system checks for disputes and applicable payment holds.
9. The companion sees the amount become eligible for withdrawal.

**Completion verification**
- Demonstrate a one-time completion PIN.
- Guest and companion must independently confirm through their authenticated accounts.
- A shared PIN alone must not be sufficient to release payment.
- If either party disputes completion, the transaction enters a review state.
- Reporting an unsafe situation must never require someone to complete a meeting or surrender their safety.

**Wallet interface**

Show two distinct balances:
- Protected booking funds: funds associated with active bookings.
- Available earnings: funds eligible for withdrawal after completion and applicable checks.

Do not build a real wallet, process actual payments, or imply that Joclarif currently holds customer money.

Production payment handling must use a legally appropriate licensed payment provider or safeguarded arrangement. The legal classification of the proposed holding and payout model must be reviewed before launch.

Explore approved profiles and make an introduction request.

**02 — Coordinate**
Confirm availability and booking details through the platform.

**03 — Confirm**
Both parties independently confirm completion, with support and dispute procedures available.

Use refined motion and visual storytelling rather than large explanatory blocks.

### 9. Protection Model — Three Perspectives

Add a compact section titled:

**Designed Around Three Layers of Trust.**

Present three elegant cards:

- **For Companions:** Identity verification, personal control, boundaries, privacy, reporting, and safety support.
- **For Guests:** Approved profiles, transparent terms, protected booking coordination, and support.
- **For the Hotel:** Visitor procedures, controlled access, incident escalation, and accountable operations.

The hotel must not be presented as automatically protected simply because the app exists. Actual protection also depends on policies, staff procedures, agreements, insurance, and legal compliance.

### 10. Demo Behaviour and Technical Requirements

- All profiles, bookings, payments, verification statuses, and transactions must be fictional simulated data.
- Add a discreet DEMO label inside the phone interface.
- No actual payment processing.
- No real identity-document collection.
- No live booking or messaging integrations.
- Use realistic state transitions so the prototype feels like a functioning product.
- Ensure the guest and companion toggles work.
- Ensure buttons, profile cards, requests, booking states, and completion screens are interactive.
- Include responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Use existing project dependencies wherever possible.
- Avoid unnecessary paid dependencies.
- Keep components modular and maintainable.

### 11. Final Quality Standard

The page should make a hotel stakeholder immediately understand:

1. What Joclarif Companion is.
2. How a guest uses it.
3. How a companion manages their participation.
4. Why verification and approval matter.
5. How booking payments and completion confirmation are intended to work.
6. How companions, guests, and the hotel are protected.

The finished experience should feel like a premium product demonstration that could be presented to an international hospitality group.

Do not build the entire production platform. Build a convincing, polished, interactive product concept that communicates the experience and operating model.

Before finishing, test the route, responsive behaviour, interactive phone flows, and existing homepage to ensure nothing else has been broken.
