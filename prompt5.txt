# J O C L A R I F — ADD MOBILITY & CAR HIRE

Extend the existing Joclarif main homepage with a premium **Mobility & Chauffeur Services** offering.

Do not redesign the homepage or disrupt existing sections. Inspect the current page and identify the most natural position for this new service.

### Product identity

Name: **Joclarif Mobility**

Headline: **Every journey, thoughtfully arranged.**

Supporting copy:

From airport arrivals to executive travel and special occasions, discover a more seamless way to move through every destination.

### Service categories

Create an elegant interactive service showcase with:

1. Airport transfers.
2. Executive chauffeur services.
3. Luxury car hire.
4. Premium SUVs.
5. Group and family transportation.
6. Intercity journeys.
7. Wedding and event transportation.
8. Hourly and full-day vehicle bookings.

### Visual presentation

Use cinematic photography of premium executive sedans, luxury SUVs, chauffeur-driven vehicles and elegant airport arrivals.

Maintain the existing STILL WATERS identity:
- Deep umber.
- Champagne gold.
- Cinematic lighting.
- Refined typography.
- Elegant transitions.
- Restrained interactions.

Do not make this resemble an ordinary car rental directory.

### Interactive experience

Build a compact booking demonstration with:

- Pickup location.
- Destination.
- Pickup date and time.
- Journey type.
- Passenger count.
- Vehicle category.
- Chauffeur preference.
- Request availability action.

The prototype should display a simulated booking request and confirmation state.

Use fictional sample vehicles and pricing, clearly labelled as demonstration data. Do not claim live fleet availability or actual provider integrations.

### Business model

Design the experience to support two future supply categories:

**Hotel fleet:** Vehicles owned or operated by the hotel.

**Verified mobility partners:** Independent vehicle owners and transport companies approved to serve Joclarif customers.

The future platform should support provider profiles, vehicle listings, booking requests, commission configuration, booking history and provider payouts.

### Homepage integration

- Add a premium Mobility section to the existing homepage.
- Make the service discoverable without overwhelming the original hotel presentation.
- Include a clear Explore Mobility interaction.
- Link to a dedicated mobility page only if the existing architecture and page density justify it.
- Ensure the feature works responsively.
- Preserve all existing homepage interactions.

### Final objective

Make Joclarif feel like a hospitality brand that can take care of the entire guest journey — not simply the room reservation.
