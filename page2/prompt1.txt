# J O C L A R I F — INTELLIGENCE
## Prompt 02: Immersive Hotel Intelligence & Operations Command Centre

### PROJECT CONTEXT

We are extending the existing Joclarif luxury hotel proposal website.

The existing project has an established visual identity called **STILL WATERS**, inspired by Charles Liu's luxury editorial aesthetic.

The visual language includes:
- Deep umber and dark brown backgrounds.
- Pale champagne gold and glowing amber gold accents.
- Cinematic lighting and atmospheric depth.
- Refined typography and editorial composition.
- Premium transitions and restrained interactions.
- Immersive 3D experiences that feel intentional rather than decorative.

The existing one-page presentation is already sufficiently populated.

**DO NOT add the Intelligence experience as another section of the existing one-pager.**

Instead, build a completely separate, dedicated page within the existing application.

Suggested route: `/intelligence`

Add a prominent, elegant entry point on the existing Joclarif homepage labelled:

**EXPLORE J O C L A R I F INTELLIGENCE**

This should navigate to the new page.

The Intelligence page must have its own navigation, experience, layout and interactive components while retaining the overall Joclarif visual identity.

---

# 1. THE PRODUCT VISION

Joclarif Intelligence is an intelligent hotel operations and business intelligence platform.

It gives authorised hotel owners, general managers and departmental managers the ability to understand the state of hotel operations at any point in time.

It should provide visibility into:

1. Financial performance.
2. Human resources and workforce.
3. Equipment, assets and maintenance.
4. Hotel operations.
5. Guest experience and service delivery.
6. Procurement, inventory and departmental performance.

The central idea:

**One intelligence layer. The entire hotel. Every important decision.**

The platform must be designed to work with both legacy and modern data.

The hotel may currently have:
- Historical spreadsheets.
- Scanned documents and paper records.
- Financial reports.
- Existing databases.
- Staff records.
- Maintenance logs.
- Existing hotel management software.
- New digital operational systems introduced over time.

The intelligence layer should eventually unify these sources.

For this proposal, use realistic simulated data. Do not claim that actual hotel integrations, databases or live connections exist.

---

# 2. THE VISUAL EXPERIENCE

The page must feel like entering a private, intelligent hotel command centre.

Do not build a generic SaaS dashboard.

Do not simply place cards, tables and charts on a dark background.

The experience should combine:

- Luxury editorial design.
- Cinematic spatial storytelling.
- Interactive 3D environments.
- Sophisticated operational visualisations.
- Functional dashboard interactions.
- Fluid transitions between different intelligence areas.

Maintain the existing Joclarif design language.

Use React Three Fiber / Three.js where appropriate, provided they fit the existing project architecture.

Prioritise performance and graceful fallbacks.

# 3. THE HOTEL COMMAND CENTRE

Create the main operational dashboard.

### Executive overview

Display a comprehensive snapshot of the hotel's current simulated operational state.

Include:

- Total revenue.
- Operating expenses.
- Estimated operating profit.
- Occupancy rate.
- Active guests.
- Staff currently on duty.
- Outstanding maintenance issues.
- Pending supplier payments.

Use believable simulated values and clearly identify the data as demonstration data.

### Interactive operational overview

Build an interactive 3D hotel representation.

This is a central feature, not a decorative background.

The hotel should be represented as an architectural 3D model with identifiable operational zones:

- Guest rooms.
- Reception and lobby.
- Restaurant and kitchen.
- Conference and event facilities.
- Housekeeping.
- Administration.
- Engineering and maintenance.
- Other relevant facilities.

Each zone should be interactive.

On hover:
- Highlight the selected area.
- Show its name.
- Show a brief operational status.

On click:
- Open an information panel.
- Display relevant simulated metrics.
- Show outstanding issues.
- Show departmental activity.
- Provide a way to navigate to the relevant intelligence section.

Example:

Selecting the restaurant should reveal:
- Department revenue.
- Operating expenses.
- Current inventory status.
- Staff allocation.
- Outstanding issues.

The hotel model must look architectural and sophisticated, not like a cartoon building.

If a complete 3D hotel model proves impractical, build a stylised, high-quality architectural cutaway with clearly separated floors and departments.

# 8. THE INTELLIGENCE ASSISTANT

This is the heart of the experience.

Create a sophisticated AI chat interface that allows authorised management personnel to ask questions about hotel operations in natural language.

The interface should feel like a serious enterprise intelligence system, not a generic chatbot.

### Interface requirements

Include:

- Conversation history.
- Natural-language input.
- Suggested questions.
- Streaming-style response animation.
- Evidence inspector.
- Related records.
- Source references.
- Department context.
- Query history.

### Example interaction

User:

"Give me an overview of the hotel's operational performance this month."

Simulated assistant response:

"The hotel generated ₦48.6 million in revenue during the reporting period, with operating expenditure of ₦29.4 million.

Occupancy averaged 76%.

The restaurant recorded the highest departmental revenue, while engineering reported three outstanding maintenance issues.

There are also five pending supplier payments."

The response should include supporting evidence.

### Evidence panel

When the user clicks "View evidence", display the records behind the answer.

Examples:
- Financial transaction records.
- Departmental reports.
- Staff attendance records.
- Maintenance logs.
- Inventory records.
- Historical documents.

Clearly distinguish:
- Verified records.
- Derived calculations.
- Historical documents.
- Simulated demonstration data.
- Missing or unavailable information.

The assistant must never pretend to know something that is not present in its available data.

If information is missing, explicitly say so.

# 9. DATA SOURCE INTELLIGENCE

This feature is particularly important.

The system must demonstrate that Joclarif can work with both legacy and modern hotel data.

Create a Data Sources workspace.

Display simulated connected sources such as:

- Historical financial spreadsheets.
- Staff records database.
- Hotel operations database.
- Maintenance records.
- Inventory management records.
- Guest management system.
- Uploaded reports and documents.

For each source display:

- Source name.
- Source type.
- Department.
- Last synchronisation.
- Number of available records.
- Connection status.
- Data freshness.

Include a visual data ingestion flow.

Show how information from different sources enters the intelligence engine, gets organised and becomes available for analysis.

Create an interactive source explorer.

Selecting a source should reveal its metadata, sample records and data coverage.

Importantly, the system should demonstrate that the intelligence layer can grow as the hotel digitises.

# 14. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Work within the existing Joclarif repository.
- Inspect the current framework and component architecture before implementation.
- Preserve all existing homepage functionality.
- Do not rebuild or replace the existing one-pager.
- Add the Intelligence experience as a separate route.
- Reuse existing design tokens, typography and visual assets where appropriate.
- Use React Three Fiber / Three.js for the interactive architectural experience if compatible.
- Use suitable charting components for financial and operational analytics.
- Make every visible control functional.
- Use realistic, internally consistent simulated data.
- Ensure the experience is responsive.
- Implement sensible loading states and empty states.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Optimise 3D assets, rendering and animation performance.
- Provide graceful fallbacks where WebGL is unavailable.

This is a hosted proposal demonstration.

Do not implement actual hotel integrations, real authentication, production database connections or live financial transactions.

However, design the interface and data architecture with the eventual production intelligence platform in mind.

---

# 15. QUALITY STANDARD

The result should feel like a premium product that a serious hotel group could imagine adopting.

It must not look like:
- A generic admin template.
- A standard Bootstrap dashboard.
- A static Figma-style mockup.
- A chatbot placed beside ordinary charts.
- A game disguised as a business platform.

It should feel like a sophisticated hotel intelligence environment.

The first 10 seconds should communicate the quality of the experience.

The next 2 minutes should demonstrate its business value.

The user should be able to explore the hotel, inspect its departments, query its intelligence assistant, examine evidence, switch roles and understand the operational picture.

**Final creative direction:**

Joclarif is not simply presenting a digital hotel.

It is presenting a hotel that can understand itself.

**J O C L A R I F INTELLIGENCE**
*See the entire operation. Understand what matters. Make better decisions.*
