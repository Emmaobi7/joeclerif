# J O C L A R I F — THE MOBILE APP EXPERIENCE & DUAL-PLATFORM SHOWCASE

## THE MISSION

We need to introduce a major improvement to the Joclarif proposal presentation.

The proposed digital transformation consists of TWO connected products:

1. A premium hotel website.
2. A dedicated Joclarif mobile application.

These are complementary products with different roles.

The website serves as the hotel's digital storefront and primary discovery and reservation channel.

The mobile application delivers a richer, more advanced and continuous guest experience.

We need the presentation to communicate this distinction clearly and immediately.

**The mobile application must become one of the most visually prominent parts of the entire proposal.**

This is not a request to build the production mobile application.

We are creating an interactive, visually compelling conceptual demonstration of the proposed mobile experience inside the existing proposal microsite.

Preserve the STILL WATERS aesthetic, existing proposal content, immersive experiences and compact presentation structure.

Do not rebuild the website.

---

## 1. THE HERO — MAKE THE MOBILE APP OBVIOUS

The hero must immediately communicate that Joclarif will have BOTH a website and a dedicated mobile app.

The mobile app should not be an afterthought.

Recompose the existing hero to introduce both products as one connected digital hospitality experience.

### Visual direction

Create a stunning cinematic hero composition featuring:

- A premium, realistic 3D smartphone as a major focal point.
- A beautifully designed conceptual Joclarif mobile application displayed on the phone.
- The phone should have realistic glass, metallic edges, reflections, depth and sophisticated lighting.
- A subtle second visual element representing the web experience, such as an architectural desktop browser composition.
- Strong editorial typography that clearly communicates the website and mobile app offering.

The smartphone should feel like a premium physical object suspended in a carefully art-directed environment.

Use cinematic lighting, controlled amber reflections and atmospheric depth.

The phone must be large enough to make the mobile app immediately recognizable.

### Suggested hero messaging

Explore a direction such as:

**"Joclarif, Beyond the Stay."**

Supporting message:

"A connected digital hospitality experience, from discovering Joclarif on the web to experiencing every moment through a dedicated mobile application."

The exact copy can be refined, but the message must remain clear.

Include two distinct visual labels:

- Joclarif Digital Experience — Web.
- Joclarif Guest Experience — Mobile App.

The visitor should understand the two-product offering without needing to scroll.

---

## 2. CREATE AN INTERACTIVE 3D SMARTPHONE SHOWCASE

This is the centerpiece of the new experience.

Build a beautiful interactive 3D smartphone that visitors can explore.

Do not simply insert a static phone mockup.

The smartphone should be a genuine interactive presentation object.

### Device interaction

Implement:

- Subtle 3D rotation following pointer movement.
- Natural perspective and depth.
- Controlled camera movement.
- Smooth transitions between application screens.
- Interactive screen hotspots or a companion feature selector.
- Touch-friendly controls on mobile.
- A reset-view interaction.

The phone should feel like a physical device being presented to the visitor.

Avoid excessive rotation or motion that makes the screen difficult to read.

### Interactive application screens

The phone should showcase a selection of conceptual Joclarif app experiences.

Create beautifully designed screen mockups for:

1. **App Home**

   A personalized guest welcome, hotel discovery, featured experiences and useful shortcuts.

2. **AI Booking Co-Pilot**

   A conversational booking experience where a guest can describe what they need naturally.

   Demonstrate how the assistant could guide room selection, dates, group requirements and booking preparation.

3. **AI Concierge**

   A guest-facing assistant for hotel information, recommendations, service requests and assistance during a stay.

   Include an illustrative conversation and a clear human handoff option.

4. **Guest Messaging**

   A dedicated conversation experience connecting guests with hotel staff.

   Demonstrate persistent conversations, message history and notifications.

5. **Digital Check-in**

   A conceptual arrival experience showing guest details, check-in preparation and supported identity-document capture.

   Clearly communicate that actual identity verification requires staff/system validation.

6. **My Reservations**

   A personalized view of upcoming and previous bookings, reservation details and guest information.

7. **Guest Services**

   An interactive service area for requests such as housekeeping, maintenance, room assistance and other hotel services.

8. **Notifications**

   A sophisticated notification centre demonstrating booking updates, check-in reminders, important guest information and service responses.

### Screen navigation

Visitors should be able to change the displayed phone screen using:

- On-screen navigation.
- Feature selectors outside the device.
- Carefully designed swipe-like transitions where practical.

The experience must be intuitive.

Do not attempt to simulate an entire production operating system.

Focus on beautifully designed app screens that demonstrate the proposed capabilities.

---

## 3. MAKE THE WEB VS MOBILE DISTINCTION EXPLICIT

We need to sell both products, but they should have distinct responsibilities.

Create a sophisticated interactive comparison experience.

Avoid a conventional comparison table.

Use a visual composition with two connected environments:

### THE WEB EXPERIENCE — DIGITAL STOREFRONT

Position the website as the primary public-facing digital destination.

Its core capabilities include:

- Hotel discovery and information.
- Accommodation and facility exploration.
- Individual reservations.
- Family and group booking.
- Event and hall enquiries.
- Essential reservation management.
- Booking enquiries and guest communication.
- Accessible contact and support channels.

The website should feel elegant, informative, accessible and conversion-focused.

### THE MOBILE EXPERIENCE — PERSONAL GUEST COMPANION

Position the mobile app as the deeper, more personalized guest experience.

Its capabilities include the web's essential guest-facing functionality, with additional mobile-first capabilities:

- Advanced AI Booking Co-Pilot.
- Personalized AI concierge.
- Richer ongoing guest conversations.
- Persistent messaging and push notifications.
- Digital check-in and arrival preparation.
- Reservation and stay management.
- Guest service requests.
- Personalized recommendations.
- Important stay updates.
- Convenient access to the guest's hospitality journey.

The app should feel like a companion that stays with the guest throughout the relationship with the hotel.

### Important

Messaging should be available through both web and mobile where appropriate.

However, the mobile application should demonstrate the richer, more continuous communication experience through persistent conversations and notifications.

Do not imply that every mobile capability is exclusively unavailable on the website.

The distinction is one of depth, continuity and experience, not arbitrary feature restrictions.

---

## 4. CREATE A VISUAL DUAL-PLATFORM DEMONSTRATION

Design an interactive presentation moment that shows how both products work together.

For example:

- A guest discovers Joclarif through the website.
- The guest makes an initial enquiry or reservation.
- The guest continues the relationship through the mobile application.
- The mobile app becomes the guest's companion before arrival, during the stay and after checkout.

Present this through a cinematic sequence.

Show a visual transition from desktop/web to smartphone/mobile.

The visitor should understand that both experiences share the same proposed digital hospitality ecosystem.

Do not implement real synchronization or backend functionality.

This is a conceptual demonstration.

---

## 5. GIVE THE MOBILE APP ITS OWN PRESENTATION CHAPTER

Introduce a dedicated mobile experience chapter in the existing compact presentation structure.

It should be one of the most engaging sections of the microsite.

The visitor should be able to:

- Explore the phone.
- Switch between application screens.
- Discover advanced mobile capabilities.
- See example guest conversations.
- Understand the difference between the website and mobile app.
- Experience the guest journey visually.

Do not make this another extremely long vertical section.

Reuse the interactive 3D presentation approach and progressive disclosure introduced in the previous redesign.

Aim for one rich presentation environment rather than eight stacked feature sections.

---

## 6. DESIGN THE APPLICATION WITH THE SAME BRAND IDENTITY

The mobile app itself must look like a real premium hospitality application.

It should have its own carefully designed UI system, while remaining visually connected to the microsite.

Use the established Joclarif palette:

- #4A3520
- #22160B
- #EFE0B9
- #F5B841
- #F7F1E6
- #80613A

Use refined typography, beautiful imagery, polished navigation and restrained accents.

The application screens must be readable and realistic at smartphone scale.

Do not simply place website screenshots inside a phone frame.

Create genuinely mobile-oriented screen compositions.

The AI interfaces should feel intelligent, warm and hospitable, not like generic chatbot templates.

---

## 7. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Work within the existing project.

Use the current framework and existing 3D infrastructure wherever practical.

For the smartphone presentation, choose an appropriate approach:

- React Three Fiber / Three.js for a true 3D device and scene.
- Optimized 3D models and materials.
- HTML/CSS-rendered screen interfaces mapped or visually integrated into the device.
- CSS 3D transformations if they provide a better performance-to-quality ratio.

The device screen should remain crisp and readable.

Avoid creating an unnecessarily complicated 3D rendering pipeline.

Lazy-load heavy assets.

Ensure mobile visitors can interact with the demonstration even if they are using the device on which the simulated phone is displayed.

For smaller screens, consider a simplified device presentation with direct screen navigation rather than relying on pointer rotation.

---

## 8. CONTENT AND SCOPE BOUNDARIES

This remains a proposal presentation.

The mobile app and web experience are proposed products.

All phone screens, conversations, bookings, notifications and hotel service interactions are illustrative demonstrations.

Do not introduce:

- Real booking integrations.
- Live payment processing.
- Real push notification infrastructure.
- Actual identity verification.
- Production chat infrastructure.
- Production mobile application code.
- Backend services or databases.

Do not invent hotel prices, room availability, facilities or operational facts.

Clearly distinguish proposed capabilities from existing hotel operations.

---

## 9. FINAL ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

After implementation, verify:

1. The hero communicates both web and mobile products immediately.
2. The smartphone is a prominent visual focal point.
3. The 3D device feels premium and interactive.
4. Visitors can explore multiple app screens.
5. Each mobile feature has a meaningful demonstration.
6. The distinction between web and mobile is immediately understandable.
7. Chat and guest communication are represented across both channels appropriately.
8. The mobile app is positioned as the more advanced guest companion.
9. The experience fits into the compact presentation structure.
10. Mobile visitors can use the demonstration comfortably.
11. The STILL WATERS visual identity remains intact.
12. No production functionality is falsely implied.

Do not stop after producing mockup images.

Implement the interactive presentation experience.

## FINAL CREATIVE DIRECTION

We are presenting two connected digital products.

The website introduces the hotel to the world.

The mobile application brings the hotel into the guest's pocket.

The website attracts, informs and converts.

The mobile application personalizes, assists, communicates and extends the guest experience.

Make that distinction visually undeniable.

**The first thing the visitor should notice is that Joclarif is being envisioned not just as a hotel with a website, but as a connected hospitality brand with its own dedicated mobile application.**
