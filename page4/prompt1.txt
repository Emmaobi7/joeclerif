## ENGINE 03 — J O C L A R I F COMPANION

Present the Companion product as a separately governed, private digital service.

Its lawful scope must be adult social companionship, introductions, and coordination for activities such as dining, events, and social outings. It must not facilitate prostitution, sexual services, trafficking, coercion, exploitation, or unlawful activity.

Show the hybrid marketplace model:

- Individuals manage their own profiles.
- Joclarif verifies identity and age.
- Joclarif approves profiles before activation.
- Only approved profiles can be discovered or receive requests.
- Guests can browse, request introductions, and coordinate lawful bookings.
- Companions retain control over availability, acceptance, boundaries, and visibility.

### Companion monetisation models

Demonstrate several potential revenue streams:

**A. Booking commission**

Joclarif earns an agreed platform commission on eligible, completed, lawful bookings.

**B. Premium membership**

Optional paid membership for additional lawful platform features, enhanced profile tools, availability management, or business analytics.

**C. Professional visibility**

Optional paid promotional placement for approved profiles, with transparent labelling and fair discovery rules.

**D. Partner services**

Potential commercial partnerships for lawful experiences, transportation, events, and hospitality services.

Make it clear that these are proposed business models, not established revenue.

### Scale and network effects

Show why the marketplace could become commercially more valuable as it grows.

More approved participants can improve choice and availability.

More guests can attract more approved participants.

More completed transactions can create recurring platform revenue.

More activity across locations can support expansion into additional cities and states.

Demonstrate the potential for a city-by-city network rather than presenting it as a single-hotel feature.

Do not imply that every profile or booking automatically generates income.

Do not include Companion revenue in the guaranteed or default ROI scenario.

All real-world operation requires appropriate legal, safeguarding, privacy, payment, and platform compliance review.

- Monthly overtime reduction.
